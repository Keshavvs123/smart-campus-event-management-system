Privacy Policy

1. Data Collection

The Smart Campus Event Management System (SMCEM) securely collects explicit user registration details, internal academic grades (CGPA), and verified date-of-birth data solely for automated criteria-checking processes handled internally by our system operators.

2. Data Usage & Security

Your payment identifiers, explicit attendance patterns, and event footprints are permanently hashed securely in our backend matrix. SMCEM heavily utilizes standard B-Crypt Password Encoding to mathematically ensure no plaintext data leaks in the event of an infrastructure breach.

3. Communication

Opt-in email notifications are rigorously maintained solely for system-level notifications concerning Event creation, modifications, and Faculty-approval communications.
