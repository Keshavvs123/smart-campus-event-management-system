About SMCEM

Smart Campus Event Management System (SMCEM) is a premiere end-to-end platform created to dramatically simplify how our university students, outstanding faculty, and brilliant organizers coordinate massive structural campus events.

Designed strictly using highly robust MVC frameworks and strict Object-Oriented design patterns, SMCEM eliminates manual event paperwork while giving organizers total control over attendee life cycles, certificate distributions, and payment integrations.

Our Mission

To digitize, secure, and infinitely scale campus communities by ensuring an incredibly dynamic, friction-free interface uniting learners to knowledge.
